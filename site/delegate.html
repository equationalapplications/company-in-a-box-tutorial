Step 6 · Delegate the work

Your agent doesn't have to do everything itself. Hermes can spawn subagents — isolated worker agents that take one task, run it, and report back. That's your worker roles from the pattern, made real.

  1. Ask your agent to hand work off, in plain language: "delegate this to a subagent: research three competitor sites and summarize each in three bullets." The delegation tool spawns an isolated worker with exactly that brief.
  2. Give several jobs at once — Hermes can run subagents in parallel, so independent tasks finish together instead of one by one.
  3. Watch the work happen: the live monitor shows every running worker, its transcript, and its cost. You can steer a worker mid-job or stop it.

Keep briefs small: a subagent sees only its brief, so the more precisely you state the job — the goal, the constraints, what "done" looks like — the better the result. This mirrors how the real company writes specs before building.
