Step 7 · Put work on a schedule

A real company doesn't wait for the owner to type a request. Hermes can run jobs on a schedule — described in plain language — so the company acts even when you're asleep.

  1. Ask your agent to schedule something, in plain language: "every weekday at 9am, check my site is up and tell me if it isn't." Hermes stores it as a scheduled job.
  2. Attach a skill if the job needs special know-how — a scheduled job can load one, so the 9am checker knows exactly how to test a site.
  3. Let results come to you: a scheduled job can deliver its output to your chat, so the company's morning report just shows up.

Start with one job: a daily "is everything healthy?" check is the classic first scheduled job at Equational Applications — memory alerts, site checks, build-status pings. One small reliable job builds more trust in the company than a week of clever ones.
