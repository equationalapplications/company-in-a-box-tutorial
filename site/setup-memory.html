Step 2 · Install Curated Thoughts

Curated Thoughts is your company's memory: a desktop app that keeps facts, decisions, and lessons in a durable, local brain your agents can search.

Two ideas are worth ten seconds before you install:

The vault is a folder of ordinary documents — anything you drop in gets watched and indexed. The brain is what the app builds from it: a local knowledge base with semantic search, so "what did we decide about X?" actually works. Both live on your machine.

  1. Download the installer for your system (macOS, Linux, or Windows) from the Curated Thoughts Releases page and install it.
  2. Open the app and pick a folder to be your vault.
  3. Drop a few documents in — PDFs, Word files, or Markdown notes. The app indexes them and proposes summary pages, which wait for your approval before they become permanent memory.

Why a human approves: facts proposed from your documents stay "pending" until you accept them. Your company's memory is only as good as what you vouch for — that's a feature, and it's why the memory stays trustworthy years later.
