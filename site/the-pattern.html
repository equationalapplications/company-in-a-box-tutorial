The pattern: one person, many roles

A normal company has people in roles. A company in a box has agents in roles — and one human who owns the whole thing.

You stay the CEO. You decide what the company should do. Agents take the roles underneath you:

  1. An executive agent holds your standing orders and runs the company day to day. It reads the company's memory, plans the work, and delegates. At Equational Applications, this agent is named Tessera — you'll meet the same idea as "your Hermes Agent" in the steps.
  2. Worker agents get one job at a time from the executive: build a page, fix a bug, research a market. They work in isolation and report back.
  3. Reviewer agents check the workers' output against the plan before anything is accepted. No self-grading: the reviewer is never the author.

Where this is heading: the same idea scales from one agent to a team that shares one memory. That is where SynapseTree comes in, at the last step.
